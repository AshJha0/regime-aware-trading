# Gate evaluation

Generated by `python tools/evaluate_gate.py --seeds 100`. Do not edit by hand; rerun the tool.

## What this shows

Over the full 2000 days at 5 bps the combined Sharpe is 0.78 unfiltered and 1.21 gated. The gate is 1 for the first 503 days, so those days do not test it. On the 1496 days it can affect (t >= 504), the Sharpe is 0.54 unfiltered and 0.99 gated, a difference of +0.456 with a 95% bootstrap interval of [-0.13, 1.05].

Holding a constant 0.625 of the book gives Sharpe 0.54, return 3.52% and max drawdown -24.15%, against 0.99, 7.02% and -15.20% for the gate. That level is the gate's own mean, known only in hindsight, so it is a control and not a rule you could have traded.

Seed 19 is one path, with 13 crisis episodes in the window against a median of 14 across seeds. Over 100 base-DGP seeds the gate beats the unfiltered book on 66% [56%, 75%] of paths, mean dSharpe +0.063, and beats the trailing vol ratio on 49% [39%, 59%], mean dSharpe -0.110. Read the bundled result and the sweep together: the bundled path alone does not settle whether the gate helps.

## Caveats

- All data is synthetic. The DGP has regimes by construction and its constants were tuned on an earlier seed, 83, so nothing here says the gate works on real markets.
- Seed 19 was picked by a rule fixed in advance: its unfiltered post-warm-up Sharpe is the closest to the median of seeds 1 to 100. No gate result entered the choice, so the gate's result on this path was not selected. It can still be unusual, and the seed sweep below says where it sits.
- Every book carries the same portfolio vol scale (10% target), computed from the unfiltered book at 5 bps. A gate multiplies that scaled book. In the cost sweep the cost moves and the scale stays fixed; in the lag sweep only the gate is delayed.
- The bundled window is 1496 days, about 5.9 years, with 13 crisis episodes (170 crisis days) starting inside it.
- The seed sweep uses 100 seeds per scenario. With that count a share has a 95% interval of up to about 10 points either side, shown in brackets.
- Windows, baselines and scenarios were chosen after the audit of the bundled result, not preregistered.
- The vol baselines have one fixed setting each and were not tuned. Their mean exposure (0.881 and 0.901) differs from the gate's.
