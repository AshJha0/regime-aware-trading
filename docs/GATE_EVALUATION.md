# Gate evaluation

Generated by `python tools/evaluate_gate.py --seeds 100`. Do not edit by hand; rerun the tool.

## What this shows

Over the full 2000 days at 5 bps the combined Sharpe is 1.80 unfiltered and 1.87 gated. The gate is 1 for the first 503 days, so those days do not test it. On the 1496 days it can affect (t >= 504), the Sharpe is 1.49 unfiltered and 1.35 gated, a difference of -0.136 with a 95% bootstrap interval of [-0.85, 0.58].

Holding a constant 0.597 of the book gives Sharpe 1.49, return 3.72% and max drawdown -3.15%, against 1.35, 3.30% and -3.41% for the gate. That level is the gate's own mean, known only in hindsight, so it is a control and not a rule you could have traded.

## Caveats

- All data is synthetic. The DGP has regimes by construction and its constants were tuned on seed 83, so nothing here says the gate works on real markets.
- The bundled window is 1496 days, about 5.9 years, with 7 crisis episodes (122 crisis days) starting inside it.
- The seed sweep uses 100 seeds per scenario. With that count a share has a 95% interval of up to about 10 points either side, shown in brackets.
- Windows, baselines and scenarios were chosen after the audit of the bundled result, not preregistered.
- The vol baselines have one fixed setting each and were not tuned. Their mean exposure (0.762 and 0.748) differs from the gate's.
